Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GTA Clips is a personal desktop tool used only by its operator with their own TikTok account.

What data is used

  • TikTok authorization tokens for the operator's own account. They are stored only on the operator's computer and are used solely to upload video drafts.
  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name) returned by TikTok Login Kit, used only to confirm which account is connected.

What is not collected

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of other TikTok users or viewers. It has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the operator's computer until the connection is revoked in TikTok settings or the local token file is deleted.

Contact

Questions can be raised by opening an issue in the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
